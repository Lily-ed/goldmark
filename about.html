ABOUT GOLDMARK CONTRACTORS LTD

WHO WE ARE

Goldmark Contractors Ltd is a private construction company based in Rwanda, offering a wide range of construction and civil engineering services.

We are involved in various construction activities, including roads, railways, utility projects and other civil engineering projects.

Our commitment to quality and efficiency enables us to meet the diverse needs of our clients while delivering reliable and professional construction solutions.

With experience across different projects, Goldmark Contractors Ltd continues to take on new challenges while maintaining a strong focus on quality and client satisfaction.
